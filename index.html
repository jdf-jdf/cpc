This class is for you if

Every group starts at the same place. Session 1 begins with opening a laptop. Nobody is expected to know anything on the first night, and nobody is asked to do anything in front of the class.

  • You have never used an AI tool

    You have heard about ChatGPT from your children, your co-workers, or the news, and you have never tried it yourself.

  • You use a phone, not a computer

    You text, you use WeChat or WhatsApp, you take photos. That is enough to start. We will cover both the laptop and the phone.

  • English is not your first language

    Instruction is available in Cantonese, Mandarin, and Spanish. You will never be graded on your English.

  • You want this for work

    You are job hunting, running a small business, or trying to keep up with what your workplace has started using.

  • You are worried about being fooled

    You have seen fake voices and fake photos and you want to know how to tell what is real. Session 5 is entirely about this.

  • You are 18 or older and live in NYC

    That is the whole eligibility list. There is no income requirement, no immigration status question, and no test.

Six evenings, six practical skills

Two hours a week. Every session is hands-on: you use a real tool on a real task of your own from the first night. Nothing is theoretical.

  1. 1

    What AI actually is, and what it is not

    A plain explanation with no jargon: what these tools do, where the answers come from, and why they sometimes make things up. You will type your first question and get your first answer before you go home.

  2. 2

    Writing things you actually have to write

    A letter to your landlord about a repair. A note to your child's teacher. A message to an employer. You bring the situation, you leave with the letter, and you learn how to make the tool sound like you rather than like a robot.

  3. 3

    Reading letters, forms, and notices

    Photograph a document and have it explained in your language. We cover what AI translation is genuinely good at, and the three kinds of paperwork — legal, medical, and immigration — where you should always confirm with a person instead.

  4. 4

    Looking for work

    Turning a job posting into plain language, building a resume from a work history you describe out loud, and practicing interview answers with a tool that will keep asking until you are comfortable.

  5. 5

    Not getting scammed

    Cloned voices on the phone, fake photos, fake job offers, and messages that seem to come from a family member. What the current scams look like, how to check before you act, and the list of things you should never type into a chatbot.

  6. 6

    Keeping it on your own phone

    Setting up free tools on your own device, with your language and your settings, before you leave the room. Plus where to come back for help: CPC runs open drop-in hours at all three centers.

What CPC provides

The course is free, and the things that usually make a free class hard to attend are covered too.

  • A laptop for every session

    Yours to use in the room. You do not need to own a computer, and you do not need to bring anything.

  • Interpretation in the room

    Cantonese, Mandarin, and Spanish interpreters are present at every session, not on a phone line.

  • A printed guide in your language

    Every step from the class, on paper, to take home. Written for someone reading it alone at the kitchen table.

  • A MetroCard for each session

    Handed out at sign-in. Two fares per class, so getting there costs you nothing.

  • Drop-in help after you finish

    Open hours at all three centers, every week. Come back with your own phone and your own question as many times as you need.

  • A CPC certificate

    Issued when you complete four of the six sessions. Several local employers and training programs recognize it.
